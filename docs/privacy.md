## 1. Introduction

G.U.Group Inc. ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to
protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we
collect, use, and safeguard your information when you visit our website or use
our services.

## 2. Information We Collect

We may collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us,
including but not limited to your name, email address, company name, and any
other information you choose to provide when contacting us or using our
services.

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to respond to your inquiries, provide our
services, improve our website and offerings, and communicate with you about
updates, news, and promotional materials (with your consent).

## 4. Data Protection

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your
personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or
destruction.

## 5. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal information. You
may also object to or restrict certain processing of your data. To exercise
these rights, please contact us using the information provided below.

## 6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or our data
practices, please contact us through our
[contact page](https://www.gu-group.com/contact).
